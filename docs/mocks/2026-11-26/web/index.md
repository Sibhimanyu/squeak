# Web app mocks (greenroom)

Static HTML, light theme (DESIGN.md Mac light neutrals, warm), system font stack. The viewer on every signed-in screen is Asha, a Member of Sibhi's greenroom. Pond tiles and avatars take colour from the pond palette; the thread duck is the real `duckThread.png` tinted by hue; the working mark is a static-pixel TideGlyph (twinkles in a browser); the sign-in mark is the real stacked lockup SVG. No coloured status dots anywhere: waiting is the amber triangle, working is the tide, unread is a bold title. Primary buttons are ink on light (yellow fails on white). Source of the files: generated by a throwaway script, so edit the HTML directly from here on.

**signin.html / signin-wide.html (390x844 / 1440x900)** - Lockup, one sentence on what Squeak is, then Continue with Zoho, Google, email as three equal outline buttons (no preferred provider, so no primary). Nothing else: no codes, no pairing.

**signin-invite.html (phone)** - Same buttons under a card: Sibhi's avatar beside the greenroom tile, "Sibhi invited you to greenroom", the address it is for and the expiry (from the invite email spec).

**ponds.html (phone)** - Title and your avatar (opens Settings). Invitations (harbour, Dev invited you, Join primary, Decline), Needs you (two questions, thread duck leading, triangle trailing), then the Pond list under the All · Shared · Not shared filter. Shared-with-me Ponds carry the owner's small avatar on the tile; unread Ponds are bold; list is newest first.

**pond-room.html (phone)** - Back to Ponds, flock avatars (invited Vikram outlined), greenroom with "Sibhi's Pond · Member · Live", Room | Ducks tabs with a pond-colour underline. Messages from people, mine right-aligned, Duck state lines collapsed to one line each (duck, name, state, time). A quiet "Neha declined" line. Composer with a "Make task" chip noting Sibhi approves Tasks.

**pond-ducks.html (phone)** - Ducks tab: Start a Duck (Member, so a note that Sibhi approves), Working group first, then Earlier. Row = thread duck, title, the agent's own line, trailing mark or age.

**duck.html (phone)** - A Duck conversation: Dev's message, the agent's reply, collapsed "Read 9 files", the line "Changed 4 files · Open on your Mac" (plain text, not a control), a collapsed ducklings line, then the decision card with lettered options (A recommended in muted words) and the composer. No Stop (Owner only).

**wide-room.html (1440x900)** - Two columns: left Invitations, Needs you, filter and Ponds (greenroom selected with a faint pond tint), account at the foot; right the open Room, 680 px reading column, composer at the bottom.

**people.html (phone, Owner view)** - Sheet over the Pond: invite field with role (Member default) and Invite; "5 of 10, counting invites"; Members with name, email under it, online / last seen text and role (Owner changes it, Remove under it); Invites with Invited · 3 days ago (Resend, Withdraw), Declined · 2 h ago and Expired (Invite again). Extra: **people-member.html** is the Member view: same sheet, no actions, a line saying only Sibhi can invite, and Leave greenroom.

**states.html (1230x640, three 360 px cards)** - Reconnecting… in the header line; Owner's Mac offline as a banner that says messages wait and arrive in order; a failure as one red sentence plus Try again under the unsent message.

**settings.html (phone)** - Account: name and email read-only with a note that they come from Zoho; Your devices (this device has no Remove, others do, with "removing removes it from every Pond"); one line "Open on your Mac" for what the web leaves out; Sign out.

## Spec conflicts and calls I made
- Ducks "working first" contradicts DESIGN 3.3 "order is never state, newest first". I followed the brief (Working group, then Earlier newest first). Decide which wins.
- The Room feed is oldest to newest with the newest at the bottom (chat), while lists are newest first.
- Spec says the web does approvals not at all; Needs you therefore holds only questions, never edit approvals.
- No dedicated Needs you screen on phone: it is a block on the Pond list, as the spec lists it.
- DESIGN 8.4 says the phone decision card has no edge; I gave it the 3 pt `subtle` edge that 3.3 gives a question, because the web has no tab saying so.
- Light theme only here; the Mac is dark primary.
